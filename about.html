About Us

Welcome to Girls in Computer Science! We're a dynamic community empowering girls in technology. Dive into coding challenges, participate in hackathons, and cultivate your skills within our supportive environment. Break barriers, challenge stereotypes, and redefine the narrative with us! Join a community where innovation and empowerment converge for limitless possibilities.
