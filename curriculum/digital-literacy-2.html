Share With Care

Intro to Digital Literacy · Module 2 of 4

Goal: think before you post — know what's safe to share and what isn't

Not everything needs to be public

Every app, game, and website you use collects some information about you. Some of that is unavoidable, but a lot of it is you choosing to share — a photo, a location tag, a birthday, a school name. Once something is posted, you lose control over who sees it, screenshots it, or shares it further, even if you delete it later.

Think before you share

Usually fine to shareThink twice before sharing
A username or gamertag (not tied to your real name)Your full name paired with your school or address
A general interest ("I like art")Your exact location or daily schedule
A public post you'd be fine with a teacher seeingPhotos that reveal your house, street sign, or license plate

A simple test: before you post, ask "Would I be okay with a stranger, a future employer, and my grandma all seeing this at once?"

Head back to Interland for this module's level.
